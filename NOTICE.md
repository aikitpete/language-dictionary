# Third-party code

`packages/client/` ships two prebuilt libraries that are not covered by this repository's MIT licence and remain under their own terms:

- `charts4j-1.3.jar` (charts4j)
- `google-api-translate-java-0.95.jar` (google-api-translate-java; it in turn bundles Hamcrest, BSD licence)

Check the upstream projects for their licence terms before redistributing these files.
